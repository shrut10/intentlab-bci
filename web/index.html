03 / FIELD NOTES

A first step towards neural interfaces.

01

The question I’m exploring

How can a noisy brain signal become a useful input to a computer? IntentLab starts with a constrained task: two imagined movements, a short recording, and the option to make no decision.

That option matters. A useful interface needs to account for uncertainty, differences between people, and changes in the signal.

02

What this experiment can tell us

It measures whether models can distinguish recorded left and right imagery cues in people they did not train on. You can inspect the full path from an open EDF recording to a deployed prediction.

Visual cues and recording artefacts may contribute to the result. These labels do not prove that a model has isolated a person’s intention.

03

Where I’d take it next

Participant-specific calibration, causal filtering for a streaming signal, and a second dataset to test transfer. Each would need a new, untouched evaluation set.

Dream reconstruction and consciousness are much larger questions. This project builds practical signal-decoding skills without claiming to answer them.

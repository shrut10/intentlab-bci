03 / RESEARCH

Methods and interpretation

01

Research question

IntentLab investigates whether models can distinguish cued left and right fist imagery in previously unseen participants, using a fixed EEG window and allowing uncertain predictions to be rejected.

The comparison includes common spatial patterns [4] and a compact convolutional model inspired by EEGNet [5], with selection based on validation participants.

02

Interpretation

The selected model reaches 61.1% participant-macro balanced accuracy, although its confidence threshold fails the validation target for reliable control, as assessed in the research report.

Visual cues, eye movements and muscle artefacts may contribute to the predictions, so classification performance alone cannot demonstrate that the model has isolated motor intention.

03

Further evaluation

A stronger evaluation would use repeated participant splits, an independent dataset and causal filtering for streaming signals, with a new untouched test set for each subsequent development.

The present results are restricted to a two-class, cued motor-imagery task and provide no evidence for decoding arbitrary thoughts, reconstructing dreams or measuring consciousness.

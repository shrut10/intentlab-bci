Inspect an operating point

Coverage is the fraction of trials accepted; selective risk is the fraction of accepted predictions that are wrong. The curve is descriptive, and inspecting test results does not validate a newly chosen threshold.

Intervals resample whole participants with the model and threshold fixed. Accepted-accuracy intervals condition on resamples containing accepted trials; undefined resamples are counted below. A 100–100% interval when every observed accepted trial was correct cannot account for unseen errors and is not evidence of perfect reliability.

What would make the evidence stronger?

This audit does not improve the decoder or establish live control. The next research priorities are a protocol evaluated on an independent dataset, preprocessing that only uses samples already received, and explicit assessment of unintended commands during rest. The research roadmap defines what each improvement would need to demonstrate.

Feedback is particularly useful on the treatment of participants who receive no commands, the limits of the bootstrap intervals and the design of an independent evaluation. A small reproduction or a concrete counterexample would help improve the project more than a general endorsement.
